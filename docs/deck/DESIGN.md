# Passersby deck design system

Inherits `../../app/DESIGN.md` (Hallmark, genre atmospheric, custom Sodium night) without change. Tokens: night #0B0E14, text #F2EBDD, sodium #FFB547 as the only accent, seeker #7FE3C4 for the verified badge only, Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono.

Deck rules on top of the app system:

- 1920x1080 slides, one slide per page in the PDF.
- Light comes from the content: lamp pools on the street scenes, the glow of the radar screenshot, the ticket. No repeated decorative dot.
- No eyebrow labels, no stat-card grids, no numbered-circle timelines, no bordered callout cards.
- No two consecutive slides share a layout.
- Real assets only: app screenshots in `../screens`, a Solana Explorer capture of a devnet encounter transaction.
